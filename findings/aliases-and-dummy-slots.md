# Renaming shells, True stubs, and unread assumption bundles

The live kernel export contains `43,135` single-node project theorem forwarding wrappers (`38,194` under `GlobalTheory.T.*` and `4,941` across other namespaces), literal `True` placeholders wired into live hypothesis bundles, and entire theorem subtrees pulled into the proof solely to populate unread struct fields. None of these breaks soundness—under Lean's kernel, ignoring an assumption or proving `True` only makes the consumer do the real work—but they make file names, theorem names, and hypothesis counts unreliable proxies for mathematical content.

## Renaming shells across namespaces

Of `38,677` live theorems in namespace `GlobalTheory.T.*` (`14,362` inside Pillar A alone), **`38,194` (`98.75%`) have `value_nodes = 1`**: their proof term is a single constant forwarding to an internal theorem under a different, often more classical-sounding name. Outside `GlobalTheory.T.*`, other project namespaces hold another **`4,941` `value_nodes = 1` theorem aliases** (`GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory` `670`, `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt` `451`, `GroundTheory.Heights₂` `407`, `Formalism.L2` `359`, `UpperTheory.Gal` `311`, `LateralTheory.Fin` `274`) plus `126` `def`s.

- [`twisted_multiplicity_one`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/LocalTypeDictionary/TwistedMultiplicityOne.lean#L30) forwards directly to `twisted_multiplicity_eq` (an equality of multiplicities under transfer, not a multiplicity-one theorem).
- [`jacquetRallisUnitMatch_all_ranks`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/JacquetRallisUnitMatchAllRanksStatement.lean#L6) is a 1-node wrapper over [`unitMatch_all`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/JacquetRallisUnitMatchAllRanks.lean#L9).
- Folder names are decoupled from content at the same scale: `Development/AbelianVarietyTheory` holds `99,187` files (`56.6%` of the corpus), yet mostly declares `_root_.GlobalTheory` (`39,212`), `IndefiniteRowSideFullHecke` (`14,165`), and `GlobalTheory.Mem` (`11,592`).
