## Executive Summary: Decoding Recursive Local Search vs. Original Mathematics

Why does a proof whose core strategy is so mathematically economical still span ~300,000 lines of code (474k lines with comments)? Auditing all 805 files reveals two sharply distinct phenomena sitting side-by-side:

1. **Deliberate, Deeply Original Mathematical Shortcuts (Preserve!):**
   Across every pillar, the proof systematically avoids classical 50k–100k LOC machinery (no Grothendieck étale/crystalline sites, no Kottwitz PEL moduli representability, no Harish-Chandra $(\mathfrak{g}, K)$-module classification, no metaplectic cover / Maslov cocycle, no Siegel Eisenstein meromorphic continuation, no endoscopy, no Local Langlands / Weil–Deligne representations, no Poitou–Tate $H^2$, no infinite Taylor–Wiles patching) by inventing concrete algebraic/analytic bypasses that fit the exact narrow interfaces needed for GRH.
2. **Artifacts of Recursive Local Goal-Search ("Dynamic Programming Around the Edges" — Refactor!):**
   Where large multi-thousand-line walls *are* unnecessarily heavy, the root cause is almost always an autonomous prover closing a local subgoal by inventing an overly general or indirect intermediate abstraction (e.g., a 2,525-LOC arbitrary-codimension flag-lowering Lie group `positionGroup` used only twice; a 583-LOC bicommutant definition of $S_B$ before proving $S_B = S[E/p]^\wedge[1/p]$; global `𝔸f E` idempotent surgery instead of placewise local-to-adelic factorization; pointwise `EdgeTuple ϑ → ℂ` corner splicing instead of monadic linear integration functionals; Galois-closure Noether normalization instead of clopen connected-component splitting) — plus **~12,000–16,000+ LOC of foundational `src/Lib/` theories (`ClassField`, `Adeles`, `Oscillator/DualPair`, `ChowlaSelberg`, `WittVector`, `DerivedComplete`, `MinkowskiSecond`, `Tauberian`, `Voronoi`) that `TauCeti` (`d9924582`, 10.2M LOC) now proves in full generality** (including ~70 `TauCeti` modules explicitly upstreamed from `grh-lean` that still have `imported_by = 0` in `src/`!).

**Realistic Whole-Repository Reduction Potential:** **~28,000–45,000 net code LOC deletable** without sacrificing readability or altering the global mathematical proof strategy.
