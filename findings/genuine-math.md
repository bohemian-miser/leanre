# Core mathematical components grounded in Mathlib types

Beneath the renaming shells and dead assumption bundles, the proof's load-bearing interfaces unfold directly to Mathlib fields, schemes, adeles, and Galois cohomology. Deep tactic bodies beyond top-level statements and wiring remain largely unread by humans; what follows is what the formal types and kernel edges actually establish.

## Windowed Sha-length bound (VWin)

[`VWin`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L49) unfolds completely to Mathlib primitives (`0` opaque leaves in its statement):

```lean
def VWin : Prop := ∀ ψ : PChar, VWinAt ψ 401
```

Through [`VWinAt`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L44), [`WinPair`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L27), [`IsChiField`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L19), and [`VVle`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L12), it states that for every primitive Dirichlet character $\psi$, every $\varepsilon \in (0, 1/52)$, and every admissible prime pair $(q, d)$ ($q \equiv 3 \pmod 8$, $d \equiv 7 \pmod{16}$), there is a multiplicative window $[Y, Y^2]$ over which the abelian CM composita $E = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2^{e(x)+2}})^+ \cdot K_\psi \cdot \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-q}) \cdot \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})$ satisfy $\sum_{l \in S} \log l \sum_{1 \le e < \lfloor x^{401} \rfloor} \operatorname{length}_{\mathbb{Z}_l}(\mathrm{Ш}^1(G_{E, S_l}, \mathbb{Q}_l/\mathbb{Z}_l(-e))) \le \exp(x^\varepsilon / 2)$. The cohomology terms [`LData`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GaloisCohomology/Statement.lean#L54), [`GS`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/Global.lean#L141), [`Sha`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GaloisCohomology/Statement.lean#L75), and [`shaLen`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GaloisCohomology/Statement.lean#L78) are built directly from Mathlib `CyclotomicField`, `jacobiSym`, `ValuationSubring`, `PadicInt`, continuous 1-cocycles, and `Module.length`.

## Étale cohomology on schemes (EtC)

Despite historical module names like `EtaleAxioms`, `GlobalTheory.EtC` constructs $l$-adic étale cohomology on Mathlib's `Scheme.etaleTopology` rather than postulating axioms:

```lean
abbrev Shv (R : Type) [CommRing R] : Type 2 := Sheaf (Scheme.etaleTopology.{0}) (ModuleCat.{1} (ULift.{1} R))
```

[`Shv`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/GalMapMul.lean#L82) and [`HQ`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/PullHlComp.lean#L239) form $\mathbb{Q}_l \otimes \varprojlim_k H^n_{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}(-, \mu_{l^k}^{\otimes i})$ via injective resolutions, with functoriality and Picard descent proved by [`etaleCohomology_functorialityAxioms_hold`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CommutativeAlgebra/EtaleCohomologyFunctorialityAxiomsHold.lean#L61) and [`b6pic_holds`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/EtaleDescentPicardGroup.lean#L250). That cohomology feeds odd-rank Galois representations in [`geoOne_holds`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/WeightOneGeometricRealization.lean#L11) (`82,643` decls), while even ranks descend via definite-unitary theta lifts in [`tLiftK`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/ThetaParseval/ThetaKernels/TLiftK.lean#L4) (`4,104` decls).

## Windowed Landau zero-free bridge

The final step from prime-sum bounds to `DirichletCharacter.LFunction χ s ≠ 0` uses neither Perron contour integration nor Turán power sums; the classical unwindowed [`ChebyshevGrowthImpliesZeroFree`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L40) draft is `0`-decl dead code. Instead, [`zeroFree_of_rpow_window`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/ZeroFreeOfRpowWindow.lean#L11), [`poleData_char`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/poleData.lean#L137), [`landau_tails`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauTails.lean#L80), and [`landau_window`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauWindow.lean#L83) implement a real-variable Laplace-derivative and Chernoff-tail localization of Landau's singularity theorem requiring only a one-sided bound $\mathrm{Gm}_\Lambda(y) \le y^\theta$ on arbitrarily large multiplicative windows $[Y, Y^{3/2}]$ (see the [full explicit-formula trace](../analysis/explicit_formula_bridge.md)).
