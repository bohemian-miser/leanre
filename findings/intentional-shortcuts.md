# Intentional shortcuts: junkEx in local Langlands and inertia-only AwayB

Rather than formalizing unrestricted local Langlands for $\mathrm{GL}_n$ and full Frobenius local–global compatibility at every prime, the proof trims both waist contracts—[`LawsT`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L55) and [`AwayB`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L26)—to the depth-zero, tame, large-residue-prime cases that downstream steps consume.

## Dummy fallback junkEx inside recTExists

[`recTExists`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/LocalCentralUnits/RecTExists.lean#L20) (`595` dominated decls) supplies a map `rc : IrrGL (w.adicCompletion E) → WD h` satisfying [`LawsT`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L55). Compared with classical local Langlands, `LawsT` omits $\varepsilon$-factors and global surjectivity, adds an explicit tame-type law ([`TypeLaw`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L35)), and guards `twist`, `central`, `dual`, `inj`, and `depth` behind `Good`—cuspidal support belonging to [`GoodCusp`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L20) (rank $1$, or smooth-character twists of depth-zero cuspidals over $\mathbb{Q}_g$).

Inside [`cuspEx`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/LocalCentralUnits/CuspEx.lean#L168), the cuspidal map branches on an [`if GoodCusp` split](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/LocalCentralUnits/CuspEx.lean#L177):

- On `GoodCusp x`, `r₀ x` computes actual $\mathrm{GL}_1$ local class field theory (via `artEx`) and Frobenius-induced tame depth-zero Weil representations over $\mathbb{Q}_g$ matching Green's regular characters.
- On `¬ GoodCusp x` (rank $n \ge 2$ of positive depth or over non-$\mathbb{Q}_g$ completions), it falls back to [`junkEx`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/LocalCentralUnits/JunkEx.lean#L257) (`55` decls), returning a single wildly ramified placeholder `J n` (`¬ IsTameWD`) twisted by an unramified character only to satisfy `absCentral`. The map is not injective on `¬ GoodCusp`.

Downstream consumers never touch that fallback. Every call site in `Row0b` inspects `rc (algNorm σ)` only under `Tor ∧ mono0` or `IsQpBig ∧ TmW ∧ Sqf ∧ mono0`:

- [`plainTor_of_tor`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/CharPolOfInert.lean#L97) proves `Tor (rc x)` forces every cuspidal constituent of `x` to have rank `1` (`GoodCusp`).
- [`mixed_of_tame`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/CharPolOfInert.lean#L151) invokes [`dz_of_cusp`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/CharPolOfInert.lean#L114)—a finite-set pigeonhole on tame labels using `Good`-restricted `inj`—to prove every cuspidal constituent has depth zero (`DZ`), hence `Good`. Because `junkEx` is wildly ramified (`¬ IsTameWD`), it cannot collide with any tame parameter.

## Inertia-only compatibility in Row0b.AwayB.tame0

At the exit of Pillar B, [`galoisRepresentations_of_thetaLift`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GaloisRepresentationsOfThetaLift.lean#L6) weakens `Row0` into [`Row0b`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L69) via [`Row0.row0b`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L84). Two explicit relaxations enter [`AwayB`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L26):

- [`IsQpBig`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L15) drops small residue primes whenever $n > 1$, requiring $E_w \cong \mathbb{Q}_g$ with $16 n^2 < g$.
- Full Weil–Deligne equality `x = y` (including Frobenius) is asserted only in [`.tor`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L29) when [`Tor`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjAlt.lean#L27) (abelian Weil image) and `y.mono0` hold. In the non-toral branch [`.tame0`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L31) (under [`TmW`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConj.lean#L18) and [`Sqf`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjAlt.lean#L29)), Pillar B proves `x.mono0` and `x.charPol g = y.charPol g` **only on inertia** (`deg g = 0`), omitting Frobenius eigenvalues for non-toral $n \ge 2$ supercuspidals. [`geoOne_holds`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/GeometricFlagBases/WeightOneGeometricRealization.lean#L11) (`82,643` decls) itself proves full unramified Frobenius `ESAt`; the inertia-only drop originates downstream in [solvable base-change restriction](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/SphericalGaloisCompatibility/PAdicSplitting/R2g.lean#L14) (see [the `geoOne_holds` audit](../analysis/geo_one_galois.md)).

## Degenerate geometry witness divXFl under anyGeometry

Across the live kernel, `57` declarations carry `junk` in their name across `16` files. Most are safely guarded totalizing sentinels (such as `junkPoint` returning `0` off-support) or `junkEx` above. One package (`23` decls) is actually constructed on real inputs: [`cfJunk`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/LatticeGeometry/InhabLoc.lean#L13), [`cxJunk`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/LatticeGeometry/Inhabited.lean#L88), and [`mdlJunk`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/LatticeGeometry/Inhabited.lean#L147) feed [`divXFl`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/LatticeGeometry/Inhabited.lean#L829) and [`geoDegenerate`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/LatticeGeometry/Inhabited.lean#L241) with `PUnit` / zero-curvature placeholder data because [`memberE_closing_alt_alt`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/MemberEClosing.lean#L17) instantiates its geometry slot with [`anyGeometry`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/OverIx.lean#L19) (`fun _ _ _ => True`). All callers of that degenerate shell stay isolated inside the `37,318`-decl `StageU` existence subtree and are unread by analytic bounds, while `x0` and `reQ` populate the real `DivX.Qp`, `MjH`, `PlP`, and `kapR` data consumed across the proof (see [the `StageU` existence audit](../analysis/stageu_existence.md)).

## Effect on soundness

None of these shortcuts creates a soundness hole: because [`everyCharacter`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/MainConclusion.lean#L10) closes the implication from `Row0b` to `EveryCharacter` inside the Lean kernel, weakening an intermediate contract only shifts work onto the consumer, which must complete the proof using those narrower invariants alone.
